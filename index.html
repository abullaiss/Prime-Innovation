Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Do you handle accounts for large housing complexes?

Yes. Housing society billing and accounts maintenance is one of our specialities. We manage billing, bookkeeping and financial reports in Tally Prime integrated with the society's bank, and we keep residents informed through a dedicated website and mobile app. Call 9830007364 for a quote.

What documents do I need for income tax filing?

Typically PAN, Aadhaar, Form 16 or salary slips, bank statements, Form 26AS/AIS, and proofs of investments and deductions. We'll share a checklist tailored to you.

Do I need to register for GST?

It depends on your business type, turnover and state, and some cases need registration regardless of turnover. Contact us for a quick assessment.

I received an income tax or GST notice. What should I do?

Don't panic. Send us a copy right away and we'll review it, prepare the response and help you meet the deadline.

Can freelancers and startups use your services?

Absolutely. We help freelancers, consultants, startups and small businesses with filing, GST, TDS and tax planning.

How much do your services cost?

Is my financial information safe?

Yes. We treat all client information as strictly confidential and use it only for your work.
